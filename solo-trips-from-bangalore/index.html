Why solo traveling with Bookmore Stays beats traveling alone

Solo travel is liberating, but planning logistics—booking remote homestays, finding reliable drivers, navigating unfamiliar roads, and dining alone—can be exhausting. Many solo travelers in Bengaluru want to get away from the city on weekends, but hesitate because of safety concerns or the friction of solitary planning.

Bookmore Stays solves this with curated community travel: you join as a solo traveler, but travel with a vetted group of 15 to 20 like-minded peers. You get all the freedom and refreshment of a weekend escape without the stress of coordinating transport, rooms, or meals.

Our weekend getaways attract tech professionals, designers, consultants, doctors, and remote workers who want to unplug from screens, make authentic real-world connections, and experience nature.

Safety, gender-balanced groups, and welcoming community vibe

Safety and comfort are our highest priorities. We maintain balanced gender ratios across our batches and enforce a strict community code of conduct.

Solo travelers are paired with roommates of the same gender in comfortable twin-sharing rooms with private attached bathrooms. Every trip is led by an experienced community trip captain who facilitates social games, ensures group safety, and coordinates all on-ground logistics.

Zero awkwardness: natural social connection through shared activities

Instead of forced corporate-style icebreakers, our itineraries feature natural social bonding activities: round-table mystery games, jeep safaris, estate treasure hunts, stargazing, and acoustic bonfire barbecue sessions.

By Sunday evening, strangers who met on Saturday morning leave as close friends with shared memories, inside jokes, and active reunion plans.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe for solo female travelers?

Yes, absolutely! Over 45% of our solo travelers are women. We ensure strict verified registrations, paired female-only room occupancy, respectful group etiquette, and vetted estate properties.

What is the average age group on these trips?

Our travelers are primarily young professionals, entrepreneurs, and creatives aged between 22 and 38 living in Bengaluru.

Do I have to participate in all group activities?

All activities are optional. If you want to spend an afternoon reading a book on the estate veranda with filter coffee, you are completely free to do so.

How does room sharing work for solo travelers?

We pair solo travelers with fellow solo travelers of the same gender in spacious twin-sharing rooms with clean, attached modern bathrooms.

What happens if a trip gets rescheduled due to weather?

If extreme weather disrupts a trip, we notify guests 48 hours in advance and offer 100% full refund or free transfer to any future weekend date.

Can I invite a friend to join with me?

Of course! While many join solo, you are welcome to bring friends, partners, or colleagues.
