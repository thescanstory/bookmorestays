Why Coorg is Bangalore's favorite 2-day weekend escape

Situated just 250 km from Bengaluru in the lush Western Ghats, Coorg (Kodagu) is renowned for its emerald coffee plantations, misty peak vistas, spice gardens, and cascading waterfalls. It offers the ideal climate and travel distance for a refreshing 2-day weekend break from Bengaluru's hectic work week.

Instead of staying in generic highway hotels or dealing with confusing taxi negotiations, Bookmore Stays provides a hassle-free, fully managed group getaway where transportation, private plantation accommodations, sightseeing permits, and delicious home-cooked meals are coordinated end-to-end.

Travelers experience the true essence of Kodava culture, sleep in tranquil heritage bungalows surrounded by pepper vines, and wake up to the aroma of freshly roasted estate coffee.

Curated itinerary highlights and outdoor adventures

Our weekend itinerary balances thrilling outdoor exploration with plenty of downtime to relax by the estate bonfire.

We bypass tourist traps to bring you to exclusive, private natural spots in Madikeri and South Coorg.

  • Mandalpatti 4x4 jeep safari: off-road ride up to the misty ridge viewpoint overlooking the Western Ghats mountain range
  • Private estate waterfall trek: hike through shaded coffee trails to a secluded natural waterfall on our private estate
  • Namdroling Golden Temple (Bylakuppe): visit one of India's largest Tibetan settlements and ornate Buddhist monasteries
  • Authentic Kodava cuisine & barbecue: savor home-cooked Pandi curry, Akki Roti, Kadambuttu, and fresh estate coffee
  • Evening acoustic bonfire: relax under starlit skies with music, barbecue skewers, and social icebreakers

Frequently asked questions

What is included in the Coorg weekend trip package?

The package includes roundtrip AC transport from Bengaluru, 1 night accommodation in a private coffee estate, 4 meals (2 lunches, 1 dinner, 1 breakfast), Mandalpatti 4x4 jeep safari charges, guided waterfall trek, and evening bonfire with barbecue.

Can solo travelers join this trip?

Yes! A large portion of our weekend travelers join solo. We pair solo travelers with roommates of the same gender in twin/triple sharing rooms, making it a wonderful way to make new friends.

Where are the pickup points in Bengaluru?

Pickups are scheduled at Indiranagar (KFC Junction), Koramangala (Sony World Signal), and Mysore Road Metro Station.

What type of food is served?

We serve home-cooked buffet spreads featuring authentic Kodava specialties (both vegetarian and non-vegetarian like Pandi curry and chicken ghee roast), hot Akki rotis, fresh vegetables, dal, rice, and desserts.

Is the Mandalpatti jeep safari safe?

Yes. We partner with experienced local 4x4 drivers who navigate the hilly off-road terrain safely while providing exhilarating panoramic views.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

We offer 100% refund on cancellations made at least 7 days prior to departure, or the option to transfer your pass to any future weekend batch.
