What is the Traitors of Coorg murder mystery experience?

Inspired by the global phenomenon of social deduction and mystery games (like Werewolf, Among Us, and The Traitors), Traitors of Coorg is India's first real-world murder mystery weekend retreat designed specifically for adventurous Bengaluru professionals, friend groups, and solo travelers.

Upon arriving at our private heritage plantation in Madikeri, players are secretly assigned their roles. By day, the entire group works together to complete thrilling physical missions, plantation treasure hunts, and estate challenges. By night, cloaked 'Traitors' covertly eliminate players one by one.

Every evening culminates in a dramatic round-table trial where participants debate, interrogate suspects, and vote to banish who they believe is betraying the team.

The setting: a secluded private coffee estate in Madikeri

Our game takes place across a 50-acre private coffee and spice estate nestled in the Western Ghats. Away from crowded tourist spots, the estate features vintage colonial architecture, high ceilings, stone fireplaces, and lush walking trails.

Guests enjoy comfortable twin-sharing heritage rooms, hot water, authentic Kodava cuisine (including Pandi curry, Kadambuttu, and vegetarian delicacies), and unlimited estate-brewed filter coffee.

  • Heritage plantation bungalow: vintage bedrooms, expansive verandas, and mist-covered estate views
  • Curated game master moderation: professional storyline hosts guide the narrative with sound effects and props
  • Private waterfall trek & jeep safari: scenic outdoor challenges integrated into the game storyline
  • Evening bonfire & barbecue: unwind after intense game trials with acoustic music and stargazing
  • AC traveler transport from Bengaluru: comfortable pickup and drop from Indiranagar and Koramangala

Solo-traveller friendly & curated community vibe

Over 60% of our participants join as solo travelers looking to disconnect from city screen fatigue and meet fun, intellectual, and open-minded people in Bengaluru.

Because the game requires communication, collaboration, and psychological strategy, awkward icebreakers are eliminated—by Saturday evening, strangers become lifelong friends bonded by shared mystery and adrenaline.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need prior experience playing murder mystery or board games?

Not at all. Our game master explains all rules, clues, and strategies during the journey. The game is designed to be intuitive, entertaining, and accessible to everyone.

Can I join as a solo traveler?

Yes! More than half of our attendees join solo. We pair solo travelers of the same gender in twin-sharing heritage rooms, and the game naturally facilitates deep friendships from hour one.

Where is the pickup point in Bangalore?

We have convenient central pickup points in Indiranagar, Koramangala, and along the Mysore Road expressway.

What meals are provided during the trip?

All meals are included: authentic Kodava and multi-cuisine buffet spreads with both vegetarian and non-vegetarian options (including local Coorg specialties), evening snacks, and fresh filter coffee.

Can we book a private edition for our company team offsite?

Yes! We run private, customized murder mystery retreats for startup teams and corporate offsites with tailored puzzle missions and leadership dynamics.

What should I pack for the weekend?

Comfortable trekking shoes, warm layers for cool Coorg evenings, a flashlight, personal toiletries, and casual outfits for the evening trials.
